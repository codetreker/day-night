# Agent Notes Instructions

Read [README.md](README.md) for the authoritative layout, format, lifecycle, and
history rules. Notes are English and record why a decision was made, its real
alternatives, and its consequences.

Before creating a note, search for one that owns the same decision. Update that
note when the decision still holds. A replacement decision gets a new note and
reciprocal links between active notes. When its predecessor is archived, link
from the new note to that archive without editing it. Keep current facts
accurate without rewriting historical rationale. Archives remain immutable.

Current work goes directly into `implemented/`; `proposed/` holds deferred work.
Scope and sequencing are decisions when their trade-offs need preserving. A note
requirement does not authorize edits during read-only research or discussion.

Use [agent-notes](../skills/agent-notes/SKILL.md) for the decision workflow,
[scope-cut](../skills/scope-cut/SKILL.md) for scope choices,
[prose-standard](../skills/prose-standard/SKILL.md) for editorial judgment, and
[trim-cot-leakage](../skills/trim-cot-leakage/SKILL.md) for removing session
narration while preserving facts.
